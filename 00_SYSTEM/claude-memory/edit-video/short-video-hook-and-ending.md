When cutting/compiling short videos from a long source video, every video must (1) open with a hook — the most attention-grabbing line (a viewer question, a surprising claim, a common mistake), pulled forward from anywhere in the source if needed — and (2) end with a clear conclusion/recommendation that resolves the problem posed, not a trailing or half-answered sentence. Length limits are flexible; completeness matters more. Don't color-grade unless asked (user cancelled grading on 2026-10-24). Never cut off the first/last word.

**Why:** User reviewed the first 5 yoga-mat shorts (2026-10-24): content flow was good but openings were flat and endings felt abrupt / half-answered.

**How to apply:** Structure each short as Hook → Body → Conclusion; reorder source segments when meaning is preserved; check the final sentence actually answers the hook's question. See [[yoga-mat-shorts-project]].
